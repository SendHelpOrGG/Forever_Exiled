ABOUT THE DATA

Live builds, skills and cluster jewels come from the same endpoints the poe.ninja builds site uses. Popularity is the share of the league ladder running a skill; DPS is poe.ninja's weighted value. Cluster combinations are counted once per build and expressed against the number of sampled builds, never the whole ladder. The snapshot loaded here is synthetic: its skill shares, DPS figures and character names are generated to demonstrate the analysis. Real numbers only appear after a sync in the Windows app.
