About the data

Live builds, skills and cluster jewels come from the same endpoints the poe.ninja builds site uses. Popularity is the share of the ladder running a skill; DPS is poe.ninja's weighted value. Combinations are counted once per build and measured against the sampled builds, never the whole ladder. The snapshot loaded here is synthetic — its shares, DPS and character names are generated to demonstrate the analysis.
