Background directions

The gallery fills the frame with the ascendancy portrait — a 152 px icon blown up to 1280 px, held at half opacity under three gradient washes. It is soft, and it says “class”, not “Path of Exile”. These are five ways to put something more of the game behind Atrium.

Closer to the game is the second pass. The first one drew geometry — even rings, ticks, uniform dots — which reads as a HUD, not as Wraeclast. PoE's language is clusters of small round nodes with bronze rims, links that bow instead of running straight, a vignetted near-black field and grain over everything, plus the game's window chrome. That is what those four are built from.

All of it is inline SVG reading the palette tokens — no new files, no upscaling, sharp at any size — so it re-tints itself in all ten palettes. The bronze is var(--gold) at low opacity, which is why it stays gold and never muddies into brown. Nothing is wired into the gallery yet: pick one and it goes into all ten frames.
