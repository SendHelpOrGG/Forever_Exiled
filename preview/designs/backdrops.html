Background directions

The gallery fills the frame with the ascendancy portrait — a 152 px icon blown up to 1280 px, held at half opacity under three gradient washes. It is soft, and it says “class”, not “Path of Exile”. Six of these seven put real Path of Exile art behind Atrium instead.

Actual art is the fourth pass and the first one that does not draw anything. The originals live in preview/assets/splash/: the official key art at 1600 px, the Labyrinth painting, the near-black character plate, and the wordmark PNG with its background keyed out to real alpha. Each direction is the same DOM — one layer holding an <img> and a palette tint — and the only things that change are the crop, the mask and whether the art is clipped into a frame. The tint is mix-blend-mode: color over desaturated art, so the picture wears the palette instead of fighting it.

The art is Grinding Gear Games’ and is here as a proposal only: it is pulled from the open web, in the same spirit as the ascendancy portraits the gallery already ships. The grove from this set is now what /designs/ ships — one plate per destination — and the rest stay here as the directions they were judged as. A fade mask keeps every crop off the card column, and the art layer sits above the mock’s gradient wash but below the panel, so the layout still owns its numbers.
