Background directions

The gallery fills the frame with the ascendancy portrait — a 152 px icon blown up to 1280 px, held at half opacity under three gradient washes. It is soft, and it says “class”, not “Path of Exile”. These are four references from the game, and the five earlier attempts kept underneath them for comparison.

From the game is the third pass, and this one starts from four specific things rather than from a mood: the title cartouche with its bust medallions, feather wings and hanging pendants; Izaro’s shield as it hangs in the Lord’s Labyrinth; the Sacred Grove, with the heart tree in its clearing, the lantern branch and the three seed colours the Harvest league is built on; and Einhar’s bestiary — cages, a lantern inside one, a net across the corner, paw prints and a capture tally. Each is drawn to its parts, not to the impression of them, and each is checked at full size so nothing is cut by the frame.

All of it is inline SVG reading the palette tokens — the metal is var(--gold) at low opacity, so it stays gold rather than muddying into brown, and the grove’s three beds are the accent, the notable and the modifier hues, which is why they re-tint in all ten palettes. Nothing is wired into the gallery yet: pick one and it goes into all ten frames.
