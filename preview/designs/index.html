Atrium — one layout, five palettes

One direction, the one that survived: Atrium — the 04 inspector panel married to the 05 cinematic cover. Full-bleed splash art, the skill and the three numbers on the left, the three combinations stacked top-down as full-width cards, and a single glass panel on the right with everything else. Nothing about the layout changes below: only the palette does.

Five dark palettes, none of them brown or green: Crimson (the original, with the pink taken out), Violet, Azure, Cyan and Slate. In every one, the notables keep one colour and the cluster modifiers another, so the two categories never blur together.

Every frame carries the whole cluster-jewel surface: share, average and peak DPS, the three combinations with their notables and cluster modifiers, socketing (size · passives · iLvl floor · price), the unique cluster jewels (Voices 21, Megalomaniac 34), the most-used notables, the three crafting routes with tries and cost, and the all-builds totals.

The frames are alive: hover anything that looks like a control — buttons, rows, tiles, tabs, the panel itself — and it lifts, glows or moves. Click a skill gem to change the build, click a combination to change the crafting target. 1:1 opens a frame at full size.
