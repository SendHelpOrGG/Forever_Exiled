Ten directions for the interface

The same screen — Winter Orb, its cluster combinations, the crafting routes — drawn ten different ways, all of them dark and none of them brown or green. Not ten colour schemes: an item inspection screen, a data grid, an editorial spread, a radial graph, a cinematic hero, a comparison page, a game HUD, a command palette, an A/B diff and a museum case. Nothing here is wired to the app. Pick a number, and I build that one for real.

Every frame carries the whole cluster-jewel surface: share, average and peak DPS, the three combinations with their notables in rose and their cluster modifiers in azure, socketing (size · passives · iLvl floor · price), the unique cluster jewels (Voices 21, Megalomaniac 34), the most-used notables, the three crafting routes with tries and cost, and the all-builds totals. No direction is allowed to drop one for looks.

The frames are alive: hover anything that looks like a control — buttons, rows, tiles, tabs, faders — and it lifts, glows or moves. Click a skill to change the build, click a combination to change the crafting target, and where a frame offers size chips they really narrow the list to Large, Medium or Small. 1:1 opens a frame at full size.
